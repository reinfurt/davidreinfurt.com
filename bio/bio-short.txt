David Reinfurt is 1/2 of Dexter Sinister, 1/4 of The Serving Library, and 1/1 of O-R-G inc. Dexter Sinister started as a small workshop on the lower east side of Manhattan and branched into projects with contemporary art institutions. The Serving Library publishes a semi-annual journal, maintains a collection of artworks, and circulates PDF texts through its website. O-R-G is a small software company. He was 2010 United States Artists Rockefeller Fellow in Design and 2017 Mark Hampton Rome Prize Fellow in Design at the American Academy in Rome. His work is in the collections of the Museum of Modern Art, Walker Art Center, Centres Georges Pompidou, and Whitney Museum of American Art. David is Professor of the Practice in Visual Arts at Princeton University.
